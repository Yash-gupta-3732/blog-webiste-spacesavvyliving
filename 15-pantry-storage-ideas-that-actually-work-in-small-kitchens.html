A well-organized pantry is the foundation of a calm kitchen. However, when you are working with limited cabinet space or a tiny reach-in pantry, things can quickly get out of hand. Finding cans, baking flours, or daily snacks becomes an exercise in frustration.

1. Clear Airtight Containers

One of the easiest ways to streamline your pantry is decanting dry goods into uniform, clear containers. This not only creates an instantly matching visual aesthetic but also lets you see exactly when you are running low on staples like flour, rice, or oats.

2. Use Hidden Door Space

Don't ignore the back of your pantry door. Over-the-door hanging racks are renter-friendly storage heroes. They add multiple tiers of shallow shelves perfect for storing spices, small jars, or canned products without taking up any internal shelf depth.

3. Categorize with Baskets

Group dry items by category (e.g., "Baking", "Breakfast", "Snacks") into natural woven or wire baskets. This makes it incredibly easy to pull out an entire basket to find what you need rather than rummaging through individual packages at the back of shelves.
