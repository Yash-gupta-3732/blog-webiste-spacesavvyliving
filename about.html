About SpaceSavvyLiving

SpaceSavvyLiving started with a 450-square-foot studio apartment, a closet that wouldn't close, and a kitchen drawer that had become a utensil black hole.

We know what it feels like to live in a space that's working against you — and we know how transformative it is when you find the right solution. That's why every article we publish, every product we recommend, and every tip we share comes from real experience organizing real homes.

We're not here to sell you a fantasy of minimalism. We're here to help you create a home that works for the way you actually live.

Our Mission

We believe that an organized home isn't a luxury — it's a foundation for a calmer, more intentional life. Our mission is to make smart organization accessible to everyone, regardless of budget, living situation, or square footage.

Whether you're a college student setting up your first apartment, a renter who can't drill into walls, or a homeowner looking to finally tackle that hall closet, we're here to help you find solutions that are practical, affordable, and genuinely effective.

Our Approach

How we research, curate, and recommend.

We Research

Every recommendation starts with deep data analysis. We compare thousands of customer reviews, analyze materials, and evaluate long-term durability to find the absolute best options on the market.

We Curate

We prioritize products that solve real problems in real apartments. We evaluate fit, ease of installation, and whether an item genuinely lives up to its claims based on extensive feedback.

We Recommend

Only products that earn our genuine endorsement make it to the site. If something doesn't work, doesn't solve a problem, or isn't worth the price, we won't recommend it — period.

Meet the Editor

Sarah Mitchell

Sarah is the editorial voice behind SpaceSavvyLiving. She believes that the constraints of small-space living are actually what spark the most creative solutions, and she leads the team in finding the very best organization strategies for our readers.

Her personal philosophy—and the philosophy of this entire site—is that every item in your home should either serve a purpose or bring you joy (preferably both!).

Instead of focusing on unattainable luxury or massive mansions, Sarah and the SpaceSavvyLiving team are dedicated to testing and sharing practical, renter-friendly, and highly effective storage solutions that work for the way you actually live.

What We Stand For

The principles behind every article and recommendation.

🤝

Honest Recommendations

We carefully select every item based purely on its practical value and customer consensus. Our editorial process is independent — affiliate commissions never influence what makes our lists.

🏠

Renter-Friendly Focus

Most of our readers rent their homes, so we prioritize solutions that don't require drilling, permanent modifications, or landlord permission. If it can't come with you when you move, we'll tell you.

✨

Quality Over Quantity

We'd rather publish one thoroughly researched article than five surface-level listicles. Every piece on SpaceSavvyLiving is written to be genuinely useful — not just to fill a content calendar.
