Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: August 2026

Transparency matters to us. We want you to understand exactly how SpaceSavvyLiving makes money, how affiliate links work, and — most importantly — why you can trust our recommendations. Here's the full picture.

How We Make Money

SpaceSavvyLiving is a free resource. We don't charge for our articles, guides, or recommendations, and we never will. To keep the Site running and support the time we invest in research, curation, and writing, we earn revenue through two primary channels:

  • Affiliate commissions: When you click a product link on our Site and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission from the retailer. This is at no additional cost to you — the price you pay is exactly the same whether you use our link or go directly to the retailer.
  • Display advertising: We display advertisements on some pages through Google AdSense. These ads are served based on your browsing history and interests, not our editorial content.

These revenue sources allow us to keep SpaceSavvyLiving completely free for our readers while investing in high-quality content.

Our Editorial Standards

Here's what we want to be absolutely clear about: affiliate commissions never influence our editorial recommendations.

Our editorial team selects every product independently. We follow a strict process:

  1. Research: We start by identifying a real organizational problem our readers face. We then research dozens of products that claim to solve it.
  2. Evaluation: We narrow the field by reading hundreds of verified customer reviews, comparing specifications, and checking long-term durability reports.
  3. Curation: We prioritize products that solve real problems. We evaluate fit, ease of installation, and whether an item genuinely lives up to its claims based on extensive customer feedback.
  4. Recommendation: Only products that pass our evaluation earn a recommendation. If a product doesn't meet our standards, it doesn't appear on the Site — regardless of potential commission.

We have declined to feature products that would have earned us commissions because they didn't meet our quality bar. We'd rather recommend nothing than recommend something we don't believe in.

What Affiliate Links Are

An affiliate link is a special URL that contains a tracking code unique to SpaceSavvyLiving. When you click one of these links and make a purchase from the retailer, the retailer recognizes that the referral came from our Site and pays us a small commission — typically between 1% and 10% of the purchase price, depending on the product category.

Here's what an affiliate link does not do:

  • It does not increase the price you pay. You pay the same price as any other customer.
  • It does not give us access to your personal information, payment details, or purchase history.
  • It does not obligate you in any way. You're free to find the same product on your own if you prefer.

Amazon Associates Program

SpaceSavvyLiving is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com and affiliated sites.

As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. Amazon and the Amazon logo are trademarks of Amazon.com, Inc. or its affiliates.

When you click a product link that leads to Amazon, you'll be taken to the Amazon website where Amazon's own Privacy Notice and Conditions of Use apply.

How It Affects You

In practical terms, our affiliate relationships affect you in one simple way: they allow SpaceSavvyLiving to exist. The small commissions we earn from product recommendations are what make it possible for us to spend dozens of hours researching, writing, and maintaining the free content you're reading right now.

If you find our content helpful and you're planning to purchase a product we've recommended, using our links is the best way to support our work. But we'll never pressure you to do so. Our goal is to be useful — everything else is secondary.

Our Promise

We believe trust is earned, not assumed. Here's what you can always count on from SpaceSavvyLiving:

  • We will never recommend a product solely because it pays a higher commission.
  • We will always disclose when a link is an affiliate link (you'll see a notice on every page that contains affiliate links).
  • We will always prioritize your trust over our revenue. If a product stops meeting our standards, we'll remove the recommendation — even if it was earning us money.
  • We will never accept payment from brands in exchange for favorable reviews or placement.
  • We will be transparent about any changes to this policy.

If you have any questions about our affiliate relationships or editorial process, we'd love to hear from you. Reach out anytime at spacesavvylivingco@gmail.com or through our contact page.
