First Apartment Essentials

Everything you need to make your first place feel like home.

Moving into your first apartment is an exciting milestone, but standing in an empty space realizing you don't even own a can opener or a shower curtain can be overwhelming. To help you transition smoothly, we've compiled the ultimate room-by-room checklist of first apartment essentials.

We prioritize quality over quantity: instead of buying massive, cheap starter packs, we recommend selecting key pieces that are durable, beautiful, and space-efficient.

1. Kitchen Essentials

You don't need professional chef gear, but you do need enough basic tools to prepare daily meals and wash up easily. Here is what to buy first:

  • A 10-inch non-stick skillet and a 3-quart saucepan with lids
  • One high-quality chef's knife and an easy-to-clean cutting board
  • A core dishware set (4 plates, 4 bowls, 4 drinking glasses)
  • Essential flatware (forks, knives, spoons) and a drawer organizer
  • A mechanical can opener, corkscrew, and measuring tools

2. Bathroom Essentials

Make your bathroom functional and spa-like immediately. Don't forget these essential items on your first night:

  • A water-resistant fabric shower curtain and plastic inner liner with hooks
  • A soft, absorbent bath mat to prevent water damage to apartment floors
  • Four high-quality cotton bath towels and two matching hand towels
  • A small vanity trash can (ideally with a step-on lid)
  • A toilet plunger and matching bowl brush (crucial for emergencies)

3. Bedroom Essentials

A good night's sleep is key when dealing with the stress of moving. Ensure your bedroom is a calm sanctuary:

  • A supportive mattress and a sturdy, squeak-free bed frame
  • Two sets of soft microfiber or cotton bed sheets
  • A cozy all-season comforter and supportive pillows with cases
  • Blackout curtains or shades to block streetlights and early morning sun
  • A pack of slim velvet hangers to maximize limited closet rods

4. Living Room Essentials

Your living area should be a cozy place to unwind. Focus on multi-functional, apartment-scale pieces:

  • A comfortable sofa that fits your space (measure doorways first!)
  • A sturdy coffee table (ideally with hidden shelf or drawer storage)
  • A floor lamp and a table lamp to create warm, ambient lighting
  • An area rug to define the space and dampen echoes for neighbors below
  • A media console table to hold your TV and organize streaming cords

5. Cleaning & Utility Essentials

Keep your new home clean and functional from the start. You'll need these cleaning staples:

  • A lightweight vacuum cleaner and a micro-fiber broom with dustpan
  • All-purpose spray cleaner, dish soaps, and laundry detergents
  • Microfiber cloths (washable and eco-friendly compared to paper towels)
  • A basic household tool set (hammer, screwdriver, measuring tape)
  • A sturdy, compact step stool to reach high apartment cabinets
