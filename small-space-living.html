How to Live Large in Small Square Footage

Living in a studio apartment, urban loft, or compact rental does not mean sacrificing comfort, style, or functionality. In fact, small spaces offer an incredible opportunity to create an intentional, curated environment where every piece of furniture and decor serves a clear purpose.

The secret to thriving in a small home lies in three core principles: zone definition, dual-purpose furniture, and vertical space utilization. By treating vertical wall height as usable real estate and ensuring every nook has a dedicated function, you prevent clutter from creeping into walkways and preserve an open, airy feeling throughout your home.
